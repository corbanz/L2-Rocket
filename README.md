# L2-Rocket
\
Scratch-built 4" high-power rocket for NAR Level 2 certification, with dual-deployment recovery.       

<img width="2131" height="636" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f2810683-214d-4382-8920-7e8167727de7" />

**Overview**\
This rocket was designed, simulated, and built from scratch to fly my NAR Level 2 certification. It uses a custom fiberglass airframe, four G10 fiberglass fins mounted through the wall, and a 54 mm motor mount. Recovery is dual deployment, controlled by a EasyMini altimeter. Several internal components are 3D printed and use brass heat-set inserts for reusable threaded connections. \
 \

**Design** \
This rocket was modeled in OpenRocket to choose possible certification motors and confirm stability, rail exit velocity, and apogee expectations. \
 \
**Avionics and Recovery** \
The EasyMini fires the drogue charge at apogee and the main charge at 1500 ft on descent. The altimeter is armed on the pad with a pullpin switch. Ejection charges were sized for each bay and verified with ground tests before flight.\
 \
Drogue: 24" Ripstop nylon chute with 0.8 C_D \
Main: 48" Rocketman Ultra-Light High-Performance with 2.2 C_D \
Shock Cord: 2x 200", 3/8" Tubular Nylon \
\
**Build** \
The custom fiberglass layup has been postponed for the certification flight to maximize altitude. The fins are mounted through the wall to the motor mount tube, then filleted on the outside.
